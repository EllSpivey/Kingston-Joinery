Kingston Joinery Schedule

Kingston Joinery operates as both a workshop and a living museum. Visitors are welcome to explore the shop, watch traditional woodworking demonstrations, and learn about early American craftsmanship. Below is our general schedule.

Monday
Business Hours
10:00am to 3:00pm

Demonstrations

Throughout the day, visitors may observe traditional woodworking techniques such as:

  • Hand‑cut dovetails
  • Traditional planing and shaping
  • Mortise and tenon joinery

Live demonstrations and educational lessons are offered all day to give visitors an inside look at traditional woodworking techniques.

Walk‑In Visits

Walk‑ins are welcome during business hours. Guests are encouraged to explore the workshop, ask questions, and learn about the woodworking traditions used between 1760 and the 1860s.

Special Events

Kingston Joinery occasionally hosts community events, educational demonstrations, and historical presentations. Future events will be listed here once scheduled.
